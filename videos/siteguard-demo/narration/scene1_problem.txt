Narrator: Every year, construction workers are injured in incidents that better monitoring could have prevented. Two causes dominate: missing hardhats, and workers wandering into restricted zones — excavations, crane swing areas, live electrical. Safety officers cannot watch every camera at once, and today's fixed alarms cry wolf so often that crews learn to ignore them. SiteGuard Agent is different: an agentic vision system that watches, reasons about risk, and changes its mind when the scene changes.
